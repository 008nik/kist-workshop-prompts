AI Agents for Customer Acquisition, hands-on 1

Prompt 1. The hypothesis to test first and two alternatives, six lines each.

Our product: Hanbit makes a clip-on battery health monitor for electric forklifts. It reads cell data at the charger and predicts a failure three weeks out, no fleet software needed. Forty warehouse operators in Korea use it. We want to sell it in Germany.

Task: Give me the hypothesis you would test first of who in Germany buys first, and at most two alternatives. For each, write six lines: segment, buyer (the role that signs), pain (in their words), primary use case, trigger (an event that makes it relevant now), kill test (what would prove it wrong).

Rules: Put the one you would test first on top and say in one sentence why. Mark every guess GUESS. No cost figures you cannot source. A trigger is an event, not proof of need.

Paste it into your AI tool as it stands to work on Hanbit. Working on your own product? Replace the "Our product" lines with two or three sentences of your own: what it does, who uses it today, and that you want to sell it in Germany.
